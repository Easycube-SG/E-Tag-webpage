**1. How It Works & Fulfillment Options**
EasyCube is built to integrate seamlessly with your existing workflow. Your team continues using your current Order Management System (OMS)—we simply automate the last-mile infrastructure with 24/7 tracking.  

**How It Works (5-Step Merchant Flow)**

1. **Merchant OMS:** Export your daily orders directly from your existing platform.  
2. **Upload CSV/XLS:** Upload the order manifest to our website dashboard before **4:00 PM daily**.  
3. **EasyCube Dashboard:** View staging statuses, collection schedules, and optimized routes in real time.  
4. **Print Shipping Label:** Print the required label containing all necessary shipping details and IDs.  
5. **Parcel Pick Up / Drop Off:** Hand over parcels via our runner pickup or drop them off at designated collection points.  

⚠️ **Important Shipping Label Note:** Shipping labels must be printed by the merchant before drop-off. Collection points are unable to assist with printing labels.  

**Required Label Details:**
• Shipping ID (Encoded in Barcode or QR code)  
• Customer Name  
• Phone Number  
• Order Date  
• Order ID 

**Shopper Collection — The Familiar Process**
EasyCube delivers a secure, hassle-free collection experience—from the moment a shopper receives their PIN to the second they walk away with their parcel.  

**The 4-Step Shopper Journey**
1. **PIN Notification:** The shopper receives a unique, secure PIN via SMS or app as soon as their order arrives at the node.  
2. **Visit Node:** The shopper visits their chosen neighborhood collection point whenever convenient.  
3. **PIN Verification:** On-site staff or kiosk verifies the PIN using our closed-loop digital handshake system.  
4. **Handover:** The parcel is retrieved, handed over, and automatically logged with an exact timestamp.
